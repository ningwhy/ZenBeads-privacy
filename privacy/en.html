Overview

ZenAura collects, stores, and transmits no personal data. The app runs fully offline, makes no network requests, has no server, no account system, and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Data we "use" (on-device only)

  • Health data (read-only): With your explicit permission, the app only reads today's step count and flights climbed via Apple Health (HealthKit) to convert them into "today's practice". The app never writes to, modifies, or shares health data. If you deny access, all features still work (a manual slider is provided).
  • Photo library (add-only): Only when you tap "Save Wallpaper", the app writes the generated image into your photo library. The app never reads, scans, or accesses any other photos.
  • Local storage: Merit counts, wishes, and outfit progress are stored on this device only (including the App Group container shared with the widget and watch app). Deleting the app removes everything permanently.

What we never do

  • Collect identity information, contact details, or device identifiers
  • Show ads, track behavior, or build user profiles
  • Transmit data to any third party (the app has no networking use case)
  • Require an account

Third-party content

The app bundles the open-source font "LXGW WenKai" (SIL Open Font License 1.1). The full license text is available in the app under Settings → Open Source Licenses.

Children's privacy

ZenAura is not marketed to children under 13 and collects no personal information from any user of any age. It is safe for everyone.
